Scope: documentation home, five section overviews, 29 focused topic views, nested navigation, legacy deep links, topic filtering, breadcrumbs, previous/next navigation, mobile access, and preserved diagrams and simulator.

Acceptance: every existing topic is reachable; one topic is visible at a time; the root is accessible from any page; old links resolve; diagrams and simulation still work; desktop and mobile navigation pass checks.

## Subsequent milestones

The next topic choices and milestone dates are intentionally pending the author's priorities. Use the section inventory to identify gaps and decide which learning paths to extend.

- **Iteration 2 — scope the content backlog:** agree on audience, learning outcome, section, prerequisites, and priority for each proposed topic.
- **Iteration 3 — publish the first topic batch:** write the selected pages, include worked examples and appropriate diagrams, review sources, and validate navigation.
- **Following iterations — extend and improve:** add selected content in small batches and review whether readers can move from concepts to business decisions.

These milestones are planning placeholders, not committed delivery dates or an exhaustive AI curriculum.
