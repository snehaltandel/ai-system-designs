02 / CLOSE THE LOOP
01

Define the decision

Name the process owner, the business outcome, and the actions AI may suggest. Record what it must never decide alone.

Measure:

Time saved, error rate, and business outcome against a baseline without AI.

02

Prove it before release

Test representative cases, missing evidence, malicious documents, and incorrect tool requests. Have domain experts judge outputs.

Release gate:

Agree on acceptance criteria before testing. Block release when a critical scenario fails.

03

Observe and intervene

Track evidence quality, rejected actions, cost, latency, and corrections. Assign someone to investigate failures.

Control:

Keep an audit trail, a pause switch, a manual fallback, and a schedule to reassess changes.

THE DESIGN RULE

The more consequential the action, the stronger the evidence, permissions, and human approval should be.
